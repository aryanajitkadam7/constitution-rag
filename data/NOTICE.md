`constitution.json` (465 articles of the Constitution of India, including the Preamble) is taken
from the open-source project [Iconlib / indianconstitution](https://github.com/Vikhram-S/Iconlib),
licensed under Apache-2.0. The text of the Constitution itself is a public government document.
Note: the source reflects the Constitution as amended over time (e.g. Article 370's entry
describes its 2019 abrogation) and omits some repealed articles.
